How to learn with this course

Mathematics is learned by doing it.

1 · Watch with a penWrite along; pause at every quick check.
2 · Solve the sheetTry all six exercises before the solutions.
3 · CompareSolution sheet or solution video, step by step.
4 · Look things upThe script uses the same numbering as the videos.

Prerequisites

A first course in linear algebra (vector spaces, matrices, determinants), sets and maps, and proof by induction. Everything else is built from scratch.
