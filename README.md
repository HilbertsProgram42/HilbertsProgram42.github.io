# Hilbert's Program – Algebra

A free university course in abstract algebra – groups, rings, modules, fields and Galois theory –
lecture by lecture, with full proofs.

**Course page:** https://hilbertsprogram42.github.io

For every episode:
- the video on YouTube (*Hilbert's Program*),
- the matching part of the script (`pdf/algebra-episode-NN-script.pdf`),
- an exercise sheet with six exercises (`pdf/algebra-episode-NN-exercises.pdf`),
- the solutions as a sheet (`pdf/algebra-episode-NN-solutions.pdf`) and as a video.

The complete script: [`pdf/algebra-script-complete.pdf`](pdf/algebra-script-complete.pdf)

Questions, suggestions or a mistake you found: comment under the video, or write to
hilbertsprogram.catering739@aleeas.com.
