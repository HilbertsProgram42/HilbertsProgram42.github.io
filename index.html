Differential Equations – a complete university course

A complete course on ordinary differential equations with full proofs and animations: solution methods, existence and uniqueness (Picard–Lindelöf, Peano, Gronwall), linear systems and the matrix exponential, phase portraits, stability and Lyapunov functions, and a final exam preparation with many solved exam problems and a mock exam. 40 chapters in five long videos; each chapter ends with six exercises, and the full solutions follow right after them.

The Riemann Theorem – from theta lifts to zeta zeros

A long film (prologue + 64 chapters, four parts) explaining, step by step, the argument for the Riemann hypothesis that Levent Alpöge announced on 20 November 2026 together with Claude and released as a Lean 4 formalization. Status: announced; the Lean code was rebuilt and kernel-checked independently the same day; formal acceptance by the mathematical community is still pending. The film follows Sahibzada Allahyar's expository reconstruction and marks every statement as proved here, classical, or an input from the Lean release. Each chapter ends with six exercises; the full solutions follow right after them.
